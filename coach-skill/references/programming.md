# Programming rules

## Level
- training age: none=0, <6 mo=1, 6–24 mo=2, >2 y=3
- beginner: training age ≤1 or mean domain score <2.5
- intermediate: training age ≥2 and mean ≥2.5
- advanced: training age 3 and mean ≥4

## Splits (days/week)
2 → Full A/B · 3 → Full A/B/C · 4 → Upper/Lower ×2 · 5 → U/L ×2 + Conditioning & core ·
6 → 5 + Zone-2 day. Exercise count by session length: 30 min→4, 45→5, 60→6, 75+→7.

## Goal prescriptions (main lifts / accessories)
| Goal | Main | Accessory | Rest | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| strength | 4×4–6 @RIR2 | 3×8–10 | 150 s / 90 s | |
| muscle & tone | 3×8–12 @RIR2 | 3×10–15 | 90 s / 60 s | |
| fat loss | 3×10–12 @RIR2 | 3×12–15 | 60 s | 8–10 min interval finisher, step goal |
| endurance | 3×12–15 @RIR3 | 2×15–20 | 45 s | circuit style |
| general health | 3×8–12 @RIR2–3 | 2–3×10–15 | 75 s | 150 min/wk moderate cardio (WHO) |

Beginners: −1 set (min 2), RIR +1.

## Mesocycle (4 weeks)
W1 baseline · W2 +1 set on main lifts · W3 RIR −1 (heavier) · W4 deload (≈60 % sets, RIR 4).
Double progression: hit top of rep range on all sets → add the smallest load next session.

## Cardio by cardio score
1: 3×20 min brisk walk (talk test) · 2: 3×25–30 min Zone 2 · 3: 3×30 min + 1 interval session ·
4–5: 2×40 min Zone 2 + 1 HIIT (6×1 min hard / 2 min easy).

## Exercise selection
Each movement pattern (squat, hinge, lunge, h-push, v-push, h-pull, v-pull, core, glute, carry,
arms, calves, cardio) has candidates tagged with tier 1–3 and equipment. Pick the highest tier ≤
the domain-driven tier whose equipment the user has; rotate candidates across sessions A/B/C.
Weak upper score → incline/knee push-ups; no pull-up bar → rows/band pulldowns.
